UEC case competition · medical tourism · body contouring

Canada can win post-weight-loss body contouring on trust, not price.

GLP-1 drugs are creating a new wave of patients who need skin removal and contouring. Many travel abroad for cheaper surgery and come home with complications. Our three linked policies make Canada the safe, coordinated choice, add capacity before volume, and return a share of revenue to Canadian healthcare.

Theory of change

Connect → Expand → Reinvest

Three policies in sequence. Each stage unlocks the next only when its evidence is in.

1Short-term · 0–12 months

Canada CareConnect

Build a trusted network connecting international patients with Canadian surgical providers and recovery services.

  • Trusted providers: verified clinics, qualified surgeons, transparent pricing
  • Patient coordination: consultations, booking, accommodation, transport
  • Recovery network: post-op support and home-doctor follow-up

Measured by bookings · satisfaction · follow-up completion · clinical safety

2Long-term · years 1–5

Healthcare Capacity Accelerator

Expand surgical infrastructure and the qualified workforce to support sustainable growth.

  • Investment incentives: paid only for proven new staff and usable OR capacity
  • Global talent: surgeons, anesthesiologists, nurses via existing pathways
  • Research partnerships: training and exchange programs

Measured by net new staff · added OR capacity · private investment · public wait times

3Long-term · years 2–5

Canada Health Dividend

Reinvest a proposed 5% of eligible international procedure revenue into measurable Canadian healthcare improvements.

  • Healthcare reinvestment: workforce training, surgical capacity
  • Financial accountability: audits, public disclosure
  • Measurable impact: continues only if it delivers verified gains

Measured by net contributions · audited spending · improvements funded · net public benefit

Price is not where Canada competes

Tummy tuck, CAD. US is ASPS 2024 surgeon fee scaled to an all-in quote.

Canada sits below a typical US all-in quote but well above Turkey and Mexico. The gap Canada closes is trust: certified surgeons, regulated facilities, and recovery that continues after the patient flies home.

Why body contouring, why now

Demand shiftGLP-1 drugs push demand from liposuction (down 11.4% in 2025) toward tummy tucks and skin removal.
Pain pointLong recoveries and complication risk make coordinated aftercare worth paying for.
ProximityToronto is a short flight from New York, Chicago and Boston, with no visa for US patients.
PrecedentSouth Korea runs a registry of 3,114 medical institutions and 1,927 patient agencies.
“Why serve international patients when Canadians already wait?”

We never give international patients priority. We start with a controlled pilot in private, accredited clinics; we add new capacity before we add volume; and a share of every procedure's revenue funds Canadian care. Expansion pauses if public access worsens.
